how to use

  1. Play the recommended word in Wordle.
  2. Enter the result as g, y, and x.
  3. Submit to get the next recommendation.
  4. Click a suggestion, or press c and choose its number.

Auto reads the visible game board. Review the guess, then press Play to send one word. If the game rejects it, choose another suggestion. The dictionary may contain words your game does not accept.
